Keyy

Learn to type with all ten fingers, without looking at your keyboard.

  1. Rest your index fingers on F and J. Both keys have a small bump you can feel.
  2. Look at the screen, not at your keyboard. The keyboard on screen shows the next key and the finger to use.
  3. After each key, move your finger back to its home key.
  4. Aim for accuracy first. Speed follows.

Lessons

Each lesson adds new keys. Reach 90% accuracy to unlock the next lesson.

Practice games

Word rain: type the falling words before they reach the red line. Both games use the keys you have unlocked.
