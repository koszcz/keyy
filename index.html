keyy

Learn to type with all ten fingers, without looking at your keyboard.

  1. Rest your index fingers on F and J. Both keys have a small bump you can feel.
  2. Look at the screen, not at your keyboard. The keyboard on screen shows the next key and the finger to use.
  3. After each key, move your finger back to its home key.
  4. Aim for accuracy first. Speed follows.
  5. Practise for 15 to 20 minutes a day.
  6. You do not need a mouse. Each button shows its key in brackets.

Your keys

You type real words made from your open keys. A key fills up as you master it. To master a key, press it 20 times with 95% accuracy at a steady pace. When you master every open key, the next key opens.

Practice games

Word rain: type the falling words before they reach the red line. Common pairs drill: practise the letter pairs that appear most often in English. Both use your open keys.
